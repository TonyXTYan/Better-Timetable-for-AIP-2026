    About this page

    An unofficial, mobile-friendly view of the AIP Congress 2026 program, built from the official Indico timetable (event 16926). Program data last updated @@RETRIEVED@@. Session and talk titles are as published; a few obvious typos were corrected.

    Dates
    7–11 December 2026, registration Sunday 6 December
    Venue
    Belinda Hutchinson Building, University of Sydney. The organisers will publish detailed rooms and locations closer to the congress, on the official site.
    Times
    All times are AEDT (Sydney, UTC+11).

    Times, rooms and abstracts can still change, so always confirm on the official pages close to the event.

    Has this page fallen behind the official program, or is something not working? Open an issue on GitHub (in a new tab), filled in with this page’s details (its address, with any search; for a problem, also your screen size, display settings and browser) for you to check before you submit.

    Tips

    Text size, font, light or dark theme, colour, contrast, motion and pop-up width. Also in the toolbar, as its sliders button.

    • Day tabs focus one day; All week shows the full program.
    • Search matches every word in any order, ignoring accents (e.g. dzurak silicon). Put “quotes” around words for an exact phrase. Matches are highlighted.
    • Short forms of institutions work too (unsw, anu, usyd). If a day has no matches, results from the rest of the week appear automatically.
    • Group badges: clicktap one (e.g. QST, NUPP) to filter to that group.
    • Star individual talks or whole sessions, then open My agenda to see them in a list or calendar, export them, or share them as a link.
    • Participants lists every speaker and convener by institution.
    • During the congress, Now jumps to what is on.
    • Keyboard: / to search, Esc to leave the search box, ← → on a day tab or in Build my agenda to step through, Ctrl or ⌘ + Z to undo a removal.
    • Shareable links: the page address keeps your day, search and filters, so you can bookmark or share a view.
    • Offline: once loaded, the page also works without a connection.
